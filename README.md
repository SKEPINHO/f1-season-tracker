F1 Season Tracker

A free desktop app for Windows and Mac that keeps a full history of your F1 25 careers. It works like a spreadsheet built for F1. Type in each result and the Drivers' and Constructors' championships update instantly. Every career is its own save with all its seasons.

It's a standalone app. It doesn't touch your game files and works offline. It only goes online to check for updates.

Download

Download from OverTake: pick the Windows or the Mac zip.

Features

Careers and seasons

Separate saves for Driver Career, My Team or your own championship
Any season year, any number of races
Your own team and driver
Transfers between seasons and mid-season driver swaps
Up to 12 teams and 24 drivers, like in the game

Tracks

All F1 25 tracks, plus classic circuits from older EA/Codemasters games
Extra circuits including Indianapolis, Fuji, Autopolis, Igora Drive and Sokol
A track card for every round with:
the country flag
a track map with sectors, turn numbers and the start/finish line
circuit length and full race distance
the real F1 lap record and your own record from all your careers

Race weekend

Qualifying formats: Full (Q1, Q2, Q3), Short or One-shot, plus Sprint Qualifying
Knocked-out drivers are filled in automatically, with locked segments and pole position
The starting grid is pre-filled into the race, including grid penalties
Race and sprint results: DNF / DNS / DSQ, fastest lap, penalties with reasons
Pit stops, tyre strategy and the two-compound rule
Weather: Dry, Mixed or Wet
Safety Car, VSC and red flags, with the laps they happened on
Race distance (5 laps, 25–100 % or custom), with laps calculated for every track

Championships and stats

Live Drivers' and Constructors' standings
A Wikipedia-style results table
Stats for poles, fastest laps, strategies, penalties, Safety Cars and qualifying tyres
Points systems from every F1 era, or fully custom points

Installation

Windows: unzip the download and run F1-Season-Tracker-…-Windows-Setup.exe. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info → Run anyway. This only happens on the first launch, because the app isn't code-signed.

Mac: unzip the download, open the .dmg and drag F1 Season Tracker into Applications. On the first launch, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

Updates

From version 1.4.0, the app checks for updates by itself:

Small updates install with one click (Update now). The app reloads in a few seconds.
Big updates that need a new installer show a Download button that opens the OverTake page.
You can also check by hand in Season settings → About → Check for updates.

Your careers are stored separately and are never touched by an update:

Windows: %APPDATA%\F1 Season Tracker
Mac: ~/Library/Application Support/F1 Season Tracker

Every update is digitally signed, and the app refuses any file that isn't.

About this repository

This repository hosts the update files the app downloads (update/). The download page with the installers is on OverTake.
